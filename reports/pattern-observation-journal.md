## Vocabulary examples

- `O0`: no odd numbers.
- `L2`: two numbers in 1–21.
- `U4`: four distinct endings.
- `C1`: one adjacent consecutive pair.
- `R1`: one number repeated from the previous calendar day.
- `Sw`: widest-minus-smallest exceeds 28.
- `B20111`: two numbers in 1–9, none in 10–19, one each in 20–29, 30–39, 40–42.

An observation can begin as a phrase such as “the endings seem to echo while the numbers move upward.” The next step is to specify which endings repeat, how much upward movement counts, and the time horizon. An unclear observation is still useful to record; it should not be scored as a successful prediction until its meaning is fixed.
